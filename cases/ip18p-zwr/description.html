No. The FixStock zip wallet case fits the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 17 Pro only. It is too small for the iPhone 18 Pro Max, which needs its own case.
Can I charge my iPhone 18 Pro without taking the zip wallet case off?
Yes. Wireless charging works through the FixStock zip wallet case, and its built-in magnets line up with MagSafe-compatible chargers. The charging port stays open for a cable.
How many cards does the iPhone 18 Pro zip wallet case hold?
The iPhone 18 Pro zip wallet case has several card slots inside the cover for everyday bank and travel cards, plus a zipped pocket for folded notes, coins, a small key or wired earphones.
Does the RFID blocking in the wallet case stop Apple Pay?
No. The RFID blocking layer only shields the cards stored in the FixStock wallet case. Apple Pay on the iPhone 18 Pro itself works as normal.

No. The outer is PU leather (faux leather) over a flexible TPU inner shell. It is soft, light and easy to wipe clean.
